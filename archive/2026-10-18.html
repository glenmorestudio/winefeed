Brazilian tropical reds keep polyphenols and health-relevant compounds through 12 months of storage

  • Researchers from Brazilian universities and Embrapa tested young Petit Verdot, Tempranillo and Syrah wines from the São Francisco Valley over 12 months of bottle storage.
  • Petit Verdot showed less colour change than wines from temperate climates, and polyphenols declined in line with the usual storage pattern.
  • Anthocyanin and trans-resveratrol levels stayed above published literature values, while the red wines softened in flavour and all wines lost some sensory character.

03IVES Open Science·15 OCT 2026

Heat treatments shift grapevine fruit set differently across four cultivars

  • In 2009/2010, Australian researchers at the University of Adelaide raised daytime vineyard temperatures 3-5 degrees Celsius using undervine tents.
  • Chardonnay under heat set less fruit, producing fewer berries and lighter bunches, while Semillon and Shiraz set more fruit.
  • Cabernet Franc showed no significant differences between heated and ambient treatments.

  • Researchers grew Grenache grafted onto three rootstocks, 110 Richter, 41-B and 161-49, in rhizotrons under two water regimes.
