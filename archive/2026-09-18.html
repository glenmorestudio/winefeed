Costco stocks boutique wines as global surplus pressures producers

  • Costco, the largest US wine retailer, now carries boutique producer bottles alongside its Kirkland Signature label, exploiting a market surplus to stock unexpected selections.

02SevenFifty Daily·7 SEP 2026

California and Pacific Northwest vineyards idle acres to cut costs during market slowdown

  • Vineyard idling, a temporary production pause, saved northern Willamette Valley producers an average of 38 percent per acre on management costs in a 2025 study of 19 sites, with some cutting costs by half.
  • Viticulturists across California, Washington, and Texas are choosing idling over removal because vines can resume high-quality production within one to three years if properly managed with irrigation, pruning, and minimal pest control.
  • Mercer Ranches in Prosser, Washington has about 1,000 of its 3,350 acres idle at various levels since 2025; producers use the pause to assess vineyard health, top graft to new varieties, or replant to different crops.

Spanish wildfires destroy 200 hectares in Sierra de Gredos wine region

  • Fires in late July destroyed 77,000 hectares across Ávila and Madrid provinces; approximately 200 hectares of vineyard in Cebreros DO, around 40 percent of registered area, were damaged by fire or smoke.
  • Bodegas Bernabeleva lost office facilities, winery sections, and equipment; Bodega El Reventón lost one single-vineyard parcel with 80 to 90 percent vine recovery expected; Comando G's Las Iruelas bottling faced heavy smoke exposure.
  • Local producers including Comando G have proposed regional subsidies for forest clearance, firebreaks, and vineyard restoration, arguing fire prevention must integrate rural development and agriculture policy.

Seven volcanic wine regions beyond Mount Etna gain recognition for complex terroir

  • Volcanic soils from past and active eruptions create layered terroir with high acidity, salinity, and savory flavors; pest resistance enables organic farming and preserves old vine varieties.
