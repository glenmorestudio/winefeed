05SevenFifty Daily·2 NOV 2026

EU greenwashing directive takes effect, restricting vague eco claims on wine

  • Directive (EU) 2024/825, also called EmpCo, bans vague environmental claims like "eco-friendly" and "green" from wine marketing and labels.
  • Enforcement began September 27, with fines up to four percent of annual turnover, applied by individual EU member states.
  • Old stock is not grandfathered in, so producers are using stickers or point-of-sale information to correct existing claims.
