03IVES Open Science·22 OCT 2026

MODIS satellite data validated for estimating vineyard water use in Italy

  • Researchers at the University of Padova developed a remote-sensing method using MODIS satellite data to map vineyard evapotranspiration in North-Eastern Italy.
  • Estimates were calibrated on two years of data and independently validated against four years of eddy-covariance measurements from a 25-hectare vineyard.
  • Results showed good consistency with in situ observations, suggesting MODIS could monitor vineyard water consumption at the regional scale.

Ethephon and manual thinning cut Portuguese Vinhos Verdes yields by up to 70%

  • A 2007 Vinhos Verdes trial tested yield control on Loureiro and Borraçal vines using chemicals and manual bunch thinning.
