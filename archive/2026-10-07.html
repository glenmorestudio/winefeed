Hungarian dry wines gain US traction, but face distribution and awareness gaps

  • US imports of Hungarian wine totalled $3.04 million in 2025, down from $5.95 million peak in 2022 but up from decade-ago levels, per UN Comtrade data.
  • Specialty importers Palinkerie, Credo Imports, and Danch & Granger report strong sales of dry Furmint, Kadarka, and Kékfrankos; sommeliers increasingly request Hungarian wines as alternatives to mainstream varieties.
  • Lack of national distributor reach and consumer awareness remain obstacles; importers and producers call for coordinated marketing behind flagship grapes like Furmint to expand on-premise placement.

Jameson releases single pot still whiskey inspired by 1826 archive recipe

  • Jameson launched Distiller's Batch, a single pot still Irish whiskey at 46% ABV and $49.99 SRP, departing from the brand's grain-spirit blend.

Arnaldo Rivera, a top tier of wines from the overperforming Terre del Barolo cooperative

  • Terre del Barolo cooperative, founded in 1958, produces 12% of all Barolo from 240 hectares of the appellation's 2200 hectares.
  • Arnaldo Rivera is the cooperative's top tier (10% of production) launched in 2017, featuring vineyard-designated wines from 35 of 300 growers paid by potential yield.
  • Terre del Barolo rejected modernist small-oak trends, using large-format wooden casks; Arnaldo Rivera uses smaller vessels with vapor-bent, non-toasted oak staves for small plot vinifications.

04The Morning Claret·Simon J. Woolf·1 OCT 2026

How Can You Train to Recognise Wine Faults?

  • Sietze Wijma founded The Art of Tasting in 2021, using synthesized flavor compounds to train wine professionals and enthusiasts to recognize faults like brettanomyces, oxidation, and volatile acidity.
  • Wijma studied psychology at Groningen University and earned a Masters in sensory science before working at a UK laboratory producing aroma compounds, which inspired his wine education business.
  • Wijma demonstrated that mousy taint cannot be smelled at normal wine pH but becomes detectable when pH lowers post-bottling, requiring only one compound (ATHP) for perception.

Red and White and Roero All Over
