Part Two of vineyard lab series examines how irrigation water shapes soil minerals

  • Viticulturist Stan Grant's second instalment in a three-part lab-analysis series covers irrigation water's role in vineyard mineral nutrition.
  • Nearly pure irrigation water, such as Sierra Nevada snowmelt, strips minerals from soils, making them more acidic and less permeable over time.
  • Saline well water high in sodium and chloride can harm soils under drip emitters and damage foliage when sprayed directly.
