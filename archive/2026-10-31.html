Enartis explains matching oak alternatives to specific winemaking goals

  • Oak chips added during fermentation barely aid colour stability because oxygen is very low at that stage.
  • Colour stability gains come mainly during maturation when oak is combined with controlled oxygen, such as micro-oxygenation.

06IVES Open Science·22 OCT 2026

MODIS satellite data used to map vineyard water use in Italy

  • Researchers at the University of Padova used MODIS satellite data to estimate evapotranspiration in a 25-hectare northeastern Italian vineyard.
  • Estimates, combined with ground weather data, were calibrated on two years of eddy-covariance measurements and validated on two independent years.
  • Results showed good consistency with in situ observations, suggesting MODIS could monitor vineyard water use at regional scale.

07IVES Open Science·22 OCT 2026

Grenache rootstocks and irrigation shape water use and stomatal control in Spain

  • IRTA researchers in Catalonia, Spain, grew three-year-old Grenache on rootstocks 110-R, 41-B and 161-49C, irrigated or non-irrigated.
  • Non-irrigated vines had higher water use efficiency, and 110-R and 41-B outperformed 161-49C.
  • Stomatal conductance differences from irrigation persisted through the season, suggesting hormonal signals outweighed hydraulic signals under these conditions.
