09SevenFifty Daily·2 NOV 2026

EU greenwashing rules restrict vague eco claims on wine labels from September 27

  • EU Directive 2024/825 (EmpCo), enforced from September 27, bans vague environmental claims such as eco-friendly and green on consumer products.
  • Wine producers must substantiate sustainability claims through third-party certification or verifiable data, and old stock is not grandfathered in.
  • Penalties are set by individual member states and can reach four percent of annual turnover for widespread violations.
