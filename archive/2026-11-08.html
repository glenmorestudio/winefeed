03SevenFifty Daily·2 NOV 2026

EU greenwashing rules restrict vague eco claims on wine labels from September 27

  • EU Directive 2024/825, known as EmpCo, bans vague environmental claims like clean, green and eco-friendly on consumer products.
  • Enforcement begins September 27, with national authorities setting penalties of up to four percent of annual turnover for widespread violations.
  • Terms like natural lack accepted definitions and are banned as generic claims unless producers substantiate them, with old stock not grandfathered.
