10SevenFifty Daily·2 NOV 2026

EU greenwashing directive takes effect, forcing wine labels to drop vague eco claims

  • EU Directive 2024/825, also called EmpCo, bans vague environmental claims like 'eco-friendly' and 'green' from wine marketing from September 27.
  • Unbacked sustainability badges and irrelevant claims are prohibited, and old stock is not grandfathered, so producers use stickers or point-of-sale notices.
  • Enforcement is left to individual member states, with fines of up to four percent of annual turnover for widespread violations.
