03IVES Open Science·22 OCT 2026

MODIS satellite data estimates vineyard water use in Italy's Veneto region

  • Padova University researchers developed a Remote Sensing method using MODIS satellite data to map vineyard evapotranspiration regionally.
  • The model was calibrated on two years of eddy-covariance measurements from a 25-hectare vineyard in northeastern Italy and tested on two independent years.
  • Estimates showed good consistency with in situ observations, suggesting MODIS could monitor vineyard water use at regional scale.

  • A 2007 trial in Vinhos Verdes tested Ethephon, Prohexadione-calcium and manual bunch thinning on Loureiro and Borraçal vines.

06IVES Open Science·22 OCT 2026

Grenache rootstocks and irrigation shape water use and stomatal control in Spain

  • Irrigation trial in a three-year-old Spanish Grenache vineyard compared three rootstocks, 110-R, 41-B and 161-49C, under irrigated and non-irrigated regimes.
  • Water use efficiency was higher in non-irrigated vines, and higher on 110-R and 41-B than on 161-49C.
  • Stomatal conductance differences from irrigation persisted through the season, and the authors suggest hormonal signals matter more than hydraulic ones.

Vine balance: how growth and fruiting trade off across the season
