08SevenFifty Daily·2 NOV 2026

EU greenwashing rules restrict vague eco claims on wine labels from September 27

  • EU Directive 2024/825, known as EmpCo, bans vague claims like "eco-friendly" and unsubstantiated sustainability badges from September 27.
  • Old stock is not grandfathered, so impacted producers are using stickers or point-of-sale information to correct claims.
  • Violations face fines of up to four percent of annual turnover, with enforcement left to individual EU member states.
