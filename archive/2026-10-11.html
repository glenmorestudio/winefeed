01Lodi Growers·8 OCT 2026

Part Two of vineyard lab series examines how irrigation water shapes soil minerals

  • Viticulturist Stan Grant's second article in a three-part series analyses lab results of vineyard irrigation water samples.
  • Nearly pure snowmelt irrigation water strips minerals from soils, making sandy, low-buffered vineyard soils more acidic and less fertile over time.
  • Alkaline well waters high in calcium and magnesium can lose fertilizer value as carbonate compounds precipitate, and saline waters can create water stress.
