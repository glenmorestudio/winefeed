01Lodi Growers·8 OCT 2026

Part Two of vineyard lab series examines irrigation water's effect on soil minerals

  • Viticulturist Stan Grant's Part Two of a three-part series covers irrigation water's role in vineyard mineral nutrient management.
  • Nearly pure irrigation water, such as Sierra Nevada snowmelt, strips soil minerals, making soils less fertile and more acidic over time.
  • Saline well water can make soils saline under drip emitters, causing grapevines to work harder to take up water and risking sodium and chloride toxicity.
