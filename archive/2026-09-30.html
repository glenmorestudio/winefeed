Seven US regions pursue AVA designations to establish wine identities

  • Seven proposed AVAs across California, Oregon, New Jersey, and New York are in early TTB approval stages; designations take several years and aim to build regional brand recognition and market differentiation.
  • Annapolis-Sea Ranch in Sonoma (26,637 acres, 651 planted) and Lake Hennessey in Napa (4,495 acres, 355 planted) emphasize Pinot Noir and Cabernet-based blends respectively, defined by fog filtering and air drainage.
  • Deschutes River in central Oregon (723,760 acres, 77 planted) and Hunterdon Hills in New Jersey (200,000 acres, 12 vineyards) represent first AVA designations for their regions, experimenting with hybrids and aromatic whites suited to their climates.

04The Wine Economist·9 SEP 2026

Costco stocks boutique wines as producers struggle to sell

  • Costco, the largest US wine retailer, stocks 100-120 wines on a rolling basis including boutique producers and its Kirkland Signature house brand, taking advantage of industry oversupply.
  • Kirkland Signature wines, launched in 2003, are small lots of around 2000 cases each made by contracted winemakers and distributed across warehouses; inventory turns over frequently to drive repeat visits.
  • Costco applies the British supermarket model (store-brand wines lending retailer credibility to unfamiliar producers) to the US market, contrasting with typical American reliance on producer brands.

05SevenFifty Daily·7 SEP 2026

California and Pacific Northwest vineyards idle acres to survive weak grape market

  • Vineyard idling, a temporary halt to production lasting one to three years, saved northern Willamette Valley producers an average of 38 percent per acre on management costs in a 2025 study of 19 sites.
  • Mercer Ranches in Prosser, Washington has idled about 1,000 of its 3,350 acres since 2025 as demand for some grape varieties collapsed; Lost Draw Vineyards in Texas High Plains used the pause to replant Pinot Grigio blocks with Picpoul Blanc.
  • Proper idling requires sustained minimal irrigation, aggressive pruning, weed control, and selective fungicide application to prevent vine damage and enable rapid return to production within two years.

Jameson releases single pot still whiskey based on 1826 recipe

  • Jameson, the world's largest Irish whiskey brand, launched Distiller's Batch, a single pot still expression bottled at 46% ABV, in fall 2025 at $49.99.

A 20 year vertical of Kumeu River’s Hunting Hill Chardonnay

  • Kumeu River's Hunting Hill Chardonnay vineyard (3.7 hectares, planted 1981-1982) was replanted entirely to clone 15 Chardonnay in 2001 after virus issues; first single-vineyard release was 2006.
  • Paul Brajkovich confirmed 2020 as perhaps the best vintage ever, scoring 97/100; 2013 frost-affected vintage yielded only 300 cases versus typical 1500.
