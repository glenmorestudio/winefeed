05IVES Open Science·22 OCT 2026

MODIS satellite data validated for mapping vineyard water use in Italy

  • Researchers at the University of Padova used MODIS satellite data to estimate evapotranspiration across a 25-hectare Italian vineyard.
  • Estimates were calibrated on two years of eddy-covariance measurements and validated against two independent years of data.

Portuguese trial finds ethephon and manual thinning cut Vinhos Verdes yields

  • A 2007 trial in two Vinhos Verdes vineyards tested ways to reduce yield in Loureiro and Borraçal vines.

07IVES Open Science·22 OCT 2026

Grenache rootstocks and irrigation trials show water-use gains in Spain

  • A three-year-old Grenache vineyard in NE Spain was grafted onto three rootstocks, 110-R, 41-B and 161-49C, and tested irrigated versus non-irrigated.
  • Non-irrigated vines had higher water use efficiency, and 110-R and 41-B outperformed 161-49C on this measure.
  • Stomatal conductance differences from irrigation persisted through the season, with a positive leaf water potential link only in non-irrigated vines.
