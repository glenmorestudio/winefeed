03IVES Open Science·15 OCT 2026

Undervine heating trial shows grapevine fruit set varies by cultivar

  • Researchers at the University of Adelaide used undervine tents to raise daytime temperatures 3-5 C in the 2009/2010 season.
  • Chardonnay under heat set less fruit, yielding fewer berries and lighter bunches, with reduced pollen tube growth.
  • Semillon and Shiraz set more fruit under heat, with higher berry counts and bunch weights; Cabernet Franc showed no significant change.
