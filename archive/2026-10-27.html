MODIS satellite data validated for mapping vineyard evapotranspiration across regions

  • University of Padova researchers Gianni Fila, Franco Meggio and Andrea Pitacco developed a remote-sensing method for mapping vineyard water consumption.
  • They used MODIS satellite data and ground meteorological observations on a 25-hectare vineyard in north-eastern Italy, with an eddy-covariance tower installed.
  • Estimates matched four years of eddy-covariance measurements, suggesting MODIS can monitor vineyard evapotranspiration at regional scale.

  • A 2007 trial in Vinhos Verdes tested Ethephon, Prohexadione-calcium and manual bunch thinning on Loureiro and Borraçal vines.

10IVES Open Science·22 OCT 2026

Vine balance explained: how growth and crop load shift through the season

  • Vine balance describes the equilibrium between a vine's vegetative growth and its reproductive growth, the fruit it bears.
  • A balanced vine ripens its crop, sets fruitful buds for the next year, and builds reserves, without excessive canopy management.
  • Standard indirect measures such as yield to pruning weight ratio do not capture how growth balances shift during the season.
