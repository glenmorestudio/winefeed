01IVES Open Science·22 OCT 2026

MODIS satellite data validated for estimating vineyard water use in Italy

  • University of Padova researchers used MODIS satellite data with ground weather observations to estimate evapotranspiration in a 25-hectare northeastern Italian vineyard.
  • Estimates were calibrated on two years of eddy-covariance measurements and independently validated on two further years, showing good consistency with in situ observations.
  • The authors conclude MODIS data could potentially monitor vineyard water consumption across a whole region, aiding water management in a densely populated, water-stressed area.

05IVES Open Science·22 OCT 2026

Grenache rootstock and irrigation trial shows lower water use and stomatal control

  • A three-year-old Grenache vineyard in NE Spain was grafted onto three rootstocks and grown irrigated or non-irrigated.
  • Non-irrigated vines had higher water use efficiency, and 110-R and 41-B outperformed 161-49 on this measure.
  • Stomatal conductance differences from irrigation persisted through the season, with stomata apparently kept open by water availability despite low leaf water potential.

08IVES Open Science·22 OCT 2026

Vine balance explained: how growth and fruiting trade off across the season

  • Vine balance is viticulture's term for balancing vegetative growth against reproductive growth in the vine.
  • A balanced vine ripens its crop, sets fruitful buds for next year, and builds reserves without excessive canopy growth.
  • Existing indirect measures such as yield-to-pruning-weight ratio do not capture how growth balances shift during the season.
