10SevenFifty Daily·2 NOV 2026

EU greenwashing directive bans vague eco claims on wine from September 27

  • EU Directive 2024/825, enforced from September 27, bans vague environmental claims like "eco-friendly" and "green" on products including wine.
  • Unverified sustainability badges and misleading claims are prohibited, with old stock not grandfathered in, so producers are using stickers or point-of-sale notices.
  • Member states set enforcement, with fines up to four percent of annual turnover for widespread violations, and wine producers must also avoid such claims in marketing.
