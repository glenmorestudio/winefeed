01SevenFifty Daily·2 NOV 2026

EU greenwashing directive now bans vague eco claims on wine labels

  • EU Directive 2024/825, known as EmpCo, took enforcement on September 27 and restricts vague environmental claims on consumer products including wine.
  • Banned claims include generic terms like eco-friendly and green, and sustainability badges without third-party certification.
  • Enforcement falls to individual member states, with fines up to four percent of annual turnover for widespread violations.

01Vineyard Magazine·29 OCT 2026

Enartis advises matching oak alternatives to specific winemaking objectives, not simply adding more oak

  • Oak alternatives differ in surface area and toast level, which control how fast and what kind of aromas they release.
  • Oak chips added during active fermentation barely aid colour stability, since oxygen is very scarce then.
  • Colour stability gains come mainly during maturation, when oak is combined with controlled oxygen such as micro-oxygenation.

  • A three-year NE Spain study compared Grenache on three rootstocks, irrigated and non-irrigated, to measure water use.

Vine balance concept and how it shifts across the growing season

  • Vine balance describes the appropriate ratio of vegetative growth to reproductive growth in a grapevine.
