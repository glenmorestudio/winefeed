Jameson releases single pot still whiskey reviving 1826 recipe

  • Jameson distiller Kevin O'Gorman at Midleton released Distiller's Batch, a single pot still Irish whiskey bottled at 46% ABV, drawing on a mash bill handwritten by John Jameson II in 1826.
  • The whiskey matures in five cask types: ex-bourbon, oloroso sherry, and virgin Irish, European, and American oak, yielding dark chocolate, spice, and silky texture.
