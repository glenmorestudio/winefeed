07SevenFifty Daily·2 NOV 2026

EU greenwashing rules now restrict vague eco claims on wine labels and marketing

  • Directive (EU) 2024/825, called EmpCo, began enforcement on September 27 and bans vague environmental claims on consumer products.
  • Terms like "eco-friendly" and "green" must be specified and substantiated, and unverified sustainability badges are prohibited.
  • Enforcement is left to individual EU member states, with fines of up to four percent of annual turnover for widespread violations.
