01SevenFifty Daily·2 NOV 2026

EU greenwashing rules take effect, restricting vague eco claims on wine labels

  • EU Directive 2024/825, enforced from September 27, bans vague environmental claims such as 'eco-friendly' and 'clean' on consumer products, including wine.
  • Non-certified sustainability badges and unsubstantiated terms like 'natural' face stricter rules, with old stock not grandfathered and fines up to four percent of turnover.
  • Enforcement is left to individual member states, with the Sustainable Wine Roundtable's Peter Stanbury warning of divergent national frameworks across 27 countries.

05IVES Open Science·22 OCT 2026

Portuguese trial cuts vine yields with Ethephon or hand thinning, varietal results differ

  • A 2007 Vinhos Verdes trial tested yield control on Loureiro (white) and Borraçal (red) vines using chemicals and manual thinning.
  • Ethephon and manual bunch thinning significantly reduced crop for both varieties, while Prohexadione-calcium had no significant effect on yield.
  • Manual thinning cut Borraçal yield about 41% versus control, and Loureiro about 26%, with quality gains only in Loureiro.

06IVES Open Science·22 OCT 2026

Grenache rootstocks and irrigation shape water use and stomatal control in NE Spain

  • A 2007-2008 Spanish study at IRTA-Torre Marimon grew three-year-old Grenache on rootstocks 110-R, 41-B and 161-49C, irrigated or not.
  • Water use efficiency was higher in non-irrigated vines, and higher on 110-R and 41-B than on 161-49C.
  • In irrigated vines stomata stayed open despite low leaf water potential, suggesting hormonal signals outweigh hydraulic ones.
