MODIS satellite data validated for mapping vineyard water use in Italy

  • Researchers at the University of Padova used MODIS satellite data to estimate evapotranspiration across a 25-hectare Italian vineyard.
  • Estimates were calibrated on two years of eddy-covariance measurements and independently validated on the other two years.
  • The results matched in situ observations well, suggesting MODIS can monitor vineyard evapotranspiration at regional scale.

05IVES Open Science·22 OCT 2026

Portuguese trial cuts Loureiro and Borraçal yields with ethephon and thinning

  • A 2007 experiment in Vinhos Verdes vineyards tested chemical and manual methods to reduce yields of Loureiro and Borraçal vines.
  • Ethephon and manual bunch thinning significantly cut crop, with manual thinning reducing Borraçal yield by about 41% against control.
  • Ethephon at berries buck-shot size raised sugar and lowered acidity in Loureiro, while Borraçal gained no quality benefit.

06IVES Open Science·22 OCT 2026

Grenache rootstocks and irrigation trials show lower water use under drought in Spain

  • Spanish researchers at IRTA-Torre Marimon grew three-year-old Grenache on rootstocks 110-R, 41-B and 161-49C, irrigated or non-irrigated.
  • Water use efficiency was higher in non-irrigated vines, and higher on 110-R and 41-B than on 161-49C.
  • Stomatal conductance stayed linked to leaf water potential in non-irrigated vines, but not in irrigated ones.
