03IVES Open Science·15 OCT 2026

Brazilian tropical vineyard study measures sunlight reaching Syrah grape clusters

  • Researchers at Embrapa Tropical Semiarid in Petrolina, Brazil, measured light reaching Syrah berries in a semiarid vineyard during the first half of 2010.
  • Photosynthetically active radiation at the top of the vineyard was about 40% of incident solar global radiation.
  • Clusters on east-facing canopy walls received more photosynthetically active light than those on west-facing walls, and leaf removal and shoot trimming changed berry light levels.

06IVES Open Science·15 OCT 2026

Undervine heating trials show grapevine fruit set rises or falls by cultivar

  • In 2009/2010, researchers used undervine tents to raise daytime temperatures 3-5°C above ambient across four grape cultivars.
  • Chardonnay under heat set less fruit, producing fewer berries and lower bunch weights, alongside reduced pollen tube growth.
  • Semillon and Shiraz under heat set more fruit, with higher berry counts and bunch weights, while Cabernet Franc showed no significant difference.
Reported byIVES Open Science: Undervine heating trials show grapevine fruit set rises or falls by cultivar
