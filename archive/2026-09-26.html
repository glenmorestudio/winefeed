New Zealand wine tariffs in India cut from October 2026, but steep barriers remain

  • New Zealand, India Free Trade Agreement takes effect October 20, 2026, reducing India's 150% wine import tariff to 25% or 50% based on bottle value, though wines under US$5 per 750ml stay at full duty.
  • Full tariff reductions will take a decade to phase in, with immediate cuts followed by smaller annual steps depending on cost, insurance and freight value.
  • State-level taxes, excise duties and outright alcohol bans across Indian states mean New Zealand exporters must navigate separate regulatory systems in each region to reach consumers.

Grape growers idle vineyards to cut costs amid weak market demand

  • A 2025 study of 19 northern Willamette Valley vineyards found idling cut average management costs by 38 percent per acre, with some sites halving expenses.
  • Vineyard idling, or mothballing production for one to three years, allows growers to preserve vines and capital rather than remove acreage when grape sales dry up.
  • Proper idling requires severe pruning, minimal but consistent irrigation, weed control, and selective pest spraying to ensure vines survive and return to quality production within one to two years.
