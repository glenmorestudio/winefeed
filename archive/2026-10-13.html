Global wine demand shifts from red toward white and sparkling styles

01Lodi Growers·8 OCT 2026

Part Two of vineyard lab series examines how irrigation water shapes mineral nutrients

  • Part Two of a three-part vineyard mineral nutrient series by viticulturist Stan Grant covers irrigation water analysis.
  • Nearly pure, low-mineral water strips nutrients from soils, making them less fertile, more acidic and less permeable.
  • Well water can carry macronutrients or micronutrients, but high alkalinity and carbonates can lock up calcium, magnesium and micronutrients.
