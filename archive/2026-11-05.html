04SevenFifty Daily·2 NOV 2026

EU greenwashing rules restrict vague eco claims on wine labels and marketing

  • EU Directive 2024/825, enforced from September 27, bans vague environmental claims like "eco-friendly" and unbacked sustainability badges on products sold in the EU.
  • Producers must substantiate claims with third-party certification or verifiable data, and old stock is not grandfathered in, so stickers are being used.
  • Fines for widespread violations can reach four percent of annual turnover, with enforcement left to each member state, creating varied national frameworks.
Reported bySevenFifty Daily: EU greenwashing rules restrict vague eco claims on wine labels and marketing
