03SevenFifty Daily·2 NOV 2026

EU greenwashing rules from September 27 restrict vague eco claims on wine labels and marketing

  • EU Directive 2024/825, also called EmpCo, bans vague environmental claims on consumer products, with enforcement starting September 27.
  • Banned terms include generic words like eco-friendly and green, plus sustainability badges lacking independent third-party certification.
  • Enforcement is left to individual member states, with fines for widespread violations of up to four percent of annual turnover.
