04SevenFifty Daily·2 NOV 2026

EU greenwashing rules now bind wine labels and sommelier talk on environmental claims

  • Directive (EU) 2024/825, called EmpCo, restricts vague environmental claims like "eco-friendly" and "green" on products sold in the EU.
  • Enforcement began September 27, with fines up to four percent of annual turnover, set country by country by each member state.
  • Terms such as "natural" must be specified and substantiated, and old stock is not grandfathered in under the rules.
Reported bySevenFifty Daily: EU greenwashing rules now bind wine labels and sommelier talk on environmental claims
