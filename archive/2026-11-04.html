02SevenFifty Daily·2 NOV 2026

EU greenwashing rules restrict vague eco claims on wine labels and marketing

  • EU Directive 2024/825, enforced from September 27, bans vague environmental claims like "eco-friendly" and unverified sustainability badges on wine.
  • Old stock is not grandfathered in, so producers are using stickers or point-of-sale information to fix existing inventory.
  • Fines can reach four percent of annual turnover, with enforcement left to each member state's own framework.
Reported bySevenFifty Daily: EU greenwashing rules restrict vague eco claims on wine labels and marketing

Roller-crimped cover crops improve Mediterranean vine water status and mycorrhizal colonization

  • A 2026 Field Crops Research study tested roller-crimped cover crops in rainfed Mediterranean vineyards.
  • Roller-crimping improved soil cover, vine water status, and mycorrhizal colonization without affecting grape quality.
  • Yield rose only in wetter seasons, while seasonal climate was the main driver of fungal diversity.
