  • Volcanic soils create high-acidity, mineral-driven wines with savory rather than fruit-forward flavors, and inhibit pests and disease, enabling organic farming across regions from Santorini to Oregon's Willamette Valley.

  • Xylella fastidiosa bacterium, which causes Pierce's disease, was detected in continental European grapevines in Portugal in 2023 and persists in regulated zones across Spain, Italy, Portugal, and France.
