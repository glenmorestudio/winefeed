01IVES Open Science·15 OCT 2026

Brazilian São Francisco Valley reds keep polyphenols high through 12-month storage

  • Researchers from Brazilian universities and Embrapa tested young tropical wines from Petit Verdot, Tempranillo and Syrah grown in Northeast Brazil's semiarid São Francisco Valley.
  • Over 12 months of bottle storage, the wines' polyphenol levels fell as expected but stayed above published levels, such as anthocyanins in Petit Verdot and trans-resveratrol in Syrah.
  • Petit Verdot showed less colour change than wines from temperate climates, and a separate sensory study found red wines softened over storage while whites stayed refreshing.

  • Adelaide researchers used passive undervine tents in 2009/2010 to raise daytime temperatures 3-5 degrees C.

  • Semillon and Shiraz showed higher fruit set and more pollen tubes entering the ovary under heat.

  • Researchers grew Grenache grafted onto 110 Richter, 41-B and 161-49 rootstocks in rhizotrons under two water regimes.
