04IVES Open Science·15 OCT 2026

Undervine heat tents reveal cultivar-dependent effects on grapevine fruit set

  • In 2009/2010, researchers used undervine tents to raise daytime temperatures 3-5 degrees Celsius over ambient in four cultivars.
  • Chardonnay under heat had lower fruit set, fewer berries and lighter bunches, with reduced pollen tube growth.
  • Semillon and Shiraz under heat set more fruit and grew more pollen tubes into the ovary; Cabernet Franc showed no significant change.
