06SevenFifty Daily·2 NOV 2026

EU greenwashing rules restrict vague wine sustainability claims from September 27

  • EU Directive 2024/825 (EmpCo) restricts vague environmental claims on consumer products, including wine, and is enforced from September 27.
  • Banned terms include unsubstantiated words like eco-friendly, green and clean, plus sustainability badges lacking third-party certification.
  • Fines for widespread violations can reach four percent of annual turnover, with enforcement left to individual EU member states.
Reported bySevenFifty Daily: EU greenwashing rules restrict vague wine sustainability claims from September 27
