Grape growers idle vineyards to cut costs as market demand slackens

  • A 2025 study of 19 northern Willamette Valley vineyards found idling cut management costs by an average of 38 percent per acre, with some halving expenses entirely.
  • Vineyard idling, a temporary suspension of production lasting one to three years, lets producers preserve assets and avoid bulldozing when grape demand collapses, unlike permanent removal.
  • Proper idling requires sustained irrigation, aggressive pruning, weed control, and selective spraying to prevent vine damage; inadequate care can take years to reverse.
